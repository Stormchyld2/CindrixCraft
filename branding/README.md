# CindrixCraft branding

Locked-in logo design: a voxel cube (3x3x3 small cubes, with four removed at the front corner so an ember-lit notch shows) used on its own as the icon and inside the wordmark, between CINDRIX and CRAFT.

## Files

These three transparent PNGs are the only raster images we use:

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `wordmark-lockup-transparent.png` | Full logo with tagline, 1760x1120 |
| `menu-header-transparent.png` | Wide header for menus, 2048x400 |
| `cube-1024-transparent.png` | Cube mark alone (icon), 1024x1024 |

Vector sources: `cube-transparent.svg` (the mark with see-through seams, used for the PNGs) and `cube.svg` (older version with a dark outline, kept only for reference).

They were exported from the design canvas, so they use the exact typeface and spacing described below. They have no outline and no background: the faces keep their colours and the seams between voxels are see-through. The off-white CINDRIX lettering and grey tagline fade out on very light backgrounds, so use them on dark or mid-tone backgrounds.

## Wordmark recipe

- Typeface: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold (800), all caps, letter-spacing 0.04em.
- CINDRIX in ash, CRAFT in amber, on one line, with the cube between them. The cube's box is 140 px tall next to 96 px type (150 px next to 100 px type on the header) and overlaps its neighbours by about 14 px each side.
- Block extrusion: five 1 px down-right offsets of the text in deep ember (four on the narrower menu header).
- Tagline: "OFFLINE VOXEL SURVIVAL", Barlow Condensed Bold, wide tracking (0.34em), in tagline grey.
